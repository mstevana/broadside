EVERYTHING ELSE

Ship hulls, hull plating, chitin carapace, nameplates, nebula cloud layers, star fields and planets are generated procedurally at runtime. Every sound effect and all eight music tracks are synthesized live with the Web Audio API. No other asset files ship with the game.

Rendering by three.js r160 (MIT), vendored for offline play.

EDGE pushes the HUD out to the left and right bezels for a wider view. A phone held sideways reserves a notch-sized strip on both edges; EDGE reclaims it, so on the side that has the notch a card may run under it. The bottom gesture strip is left alone either way.

CONTROLS

Tap open space — move selected ships on the horizontal plane.

Press + drag up/down — set the move point, then its altitude. Release to commit.

Tap an enemy — designate it as the target for selected ships.

Two fingers — orbit the camera. Pinch — zoom. (Mouse: right-drag / wheel.)

Tap a subsystem chip on the target panel — focus precision fire on that device (its shields must be down).

THE KILL CHAIN

ANTI-SHIELD energy weapons crack the deflector but do nothing to hull.

ANTI-HULL railguns & heavy lasers smash hull — mostly wasted against shields. Torpedoes partially bleed through.

ANTI-DEVICE precision weapons disable engines, shield generators, sensors, mounts.

Power is the real limit: even a top reactor sustains only 3–4 weapons at full rate. Use the PWR panel to trade fire rate, shield regen, thrust and sensor reach.
